Pin map (atmega328):
0   Serial RX (MIDI in), also connected to FTDI TX
1   Serial TX, connected to FTDI RX
2   YM2612 D2
3   YM2612 clock
4   YM2612 D4
5   YM2612 D5
6   YM2612 D6
7   YM2612 D7
8   YM2612 D0
9   YM2612 D1
10  Op2 panel mod LED
11  YM2612 D3
12  Op3 panel mod LED
13  Op1 panel mod LED
A0  -
A1  YM2612 ~CS
A2  YM2612 ~WR
A3  YM2612 ~RD
A4  YM2612 A0
A5  YM2612 A1

Port bit masks corresponding to YM2612 data pins:
PORTB &= ~0x0b      // 00001011
PORTD &= ~0xf4      // 11110100
PORTB |= data & 0x0b
PORTD |= data & 0xf4

Split is necessary to accommodate UART and YM2612 clock
